# Launch channels

Ordered by what a hyperlocal daily puzzle actually needs, which is not
reach — it is the right thousand people. A puzzle about Fremont is
worthless to somebody in Ohio and close to essential to somebody who
drives past Mission Peak every morning.

Everything below is written to be sent. Fill in your name and phone and
go; nothing here needs rewriting first.

## The one advantage you have

You are a Fremont teacher who built a Fremont thing, and it is free with
no advertising on it. That is the story, and it is why local press will
run it and community groups will not treat you as spam. Lead with it
every time. Do not describe yourself as a startup.

---

## Day 1 · Nextdoor

Highest concentration of the exact audience, and it costs nothing. Post
from your own account in your own neighborhood, then share to nearby
ones. Attach `square-1080.png`.

> I teach here in Fremont and I've spent the last few months building a
> little daily puzzle about the town. Five short ones every morning — name
> a place from a drawing, sort twelve local things into groups, guess what
> year a scene is from.
>
> It's free, there's nothing to install, and there are no ads on it. I
> made it because my students know this town and I couldn't find anything
> that treated it as worth knowing.
>
> fremontgame.vercel.app
>
> Today's asks you to name a place from a drawing. I'd be curious how many
> people get it.

Then reply to every comment for a week. That is the whole game on
Nextdoor — the replies are what push a post back up the feed.

## Day 1 · Patch (self-serve, free)

Fremont Patch takes community posts directly, no editor to persuade:

1. Make an account at **support.patch.com** if you don't have one — pick
   "create an account", say why you want to post.
2. Post at **contribute.patch.com**, filed to Fremont.

Use the press text below as the body. Patch posts are indexed by Google,
which is worth more over a year than the day's traffic.

## Day 2 · Tri-City Voice

The Fremont/Newark/Union City weekly. Small enough to answer, and it
covers exactly this sort of thing.

- **assignmenteditor@tricityvoice.com** — press releases and story ideas
- (510) 494-1999 · 39737 Paseo Padre Pkwy, Fremont CA 94538
- Mon–Fri 9–5. A phone call after a couple of days is normal here, not

## Day 2 · Fremont Chamber of Commerce

- **fmtcc@fremontbusiness.com** · (510) 795-2244

## Week 2 · The rest

- **East Bay Times / The Argus** — bigger and slower. Worth one email
  once you have a Tri-City Voice piece to point at.
- **r/Fremont** — read the rules first. Some subs ban self-promotion
  outright, and posting anyway gets you banned rather than downvoted.
- **Facebook** — the Fremont community groups and the "grew up in
  Fremont" nostalgia groups. The nostalgia groups are the best fit you
  will find anywhere; Then & Now is built for exactly that audience.
- **Fremont Main Library** and the **Niles Essanay Silent Film Museum** —
  both have engaged local followings and no reason to compete with you.
- **Your school.** Ask whoever owns a channel before posting to it. This
  got much simpler when the site stopped selling anything: a free local
  history puzzle with no advertising is the kind of thing a district
  newsletter exists to carry.

## What not to do

- **Do not buy ads.** You do not know your retention yet. Paid traffic
  into a leaky bucket buys a number and teaches you nothing.
- **Do not post everywhere on one day.** Nextdoor and Patch first, watch
  for a week, then widen. If the first day goes badly you want to have
  spent one channel, not six.
- **Do not send at the weekend.** Tri-City Voice and the Chamber are
  weekday offices. Tuesday morning is the best slot you have.
